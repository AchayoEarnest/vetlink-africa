Describe what you see. VetLink AI checks for warning signs and suggests how soon to involve a vet. It never diagnoses.

My dog has been vomiting several times and is not eating.
Vomiting can have many causes. Because repeated vomiting can lead to dehydration, consider contacting a veterinarian, especially if your dog is weak, has blood in the vomit, or cannot keep water down.

Emergency animal care

Call a vet now. Describe the animal, the problem and your location.

  1. Keep the animal calm, warm and away from other animals.
  2. Do not give human medicine or force food or water.
  3. For bleeding, press firmly with a clean cloth.
  4. If poisoning is suspected, keep the container or plant to show the vet.
